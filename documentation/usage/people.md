# People

The people section holds the family members - and the others who matter to the archive, like a photographer or a columnist. Each person has a page with what's known about them, their family, and the photos, documents and events they appear in.

## The people list

**People** in the header lists everyone, A to Z by last name. Search by name, and choose who's shown:

- **Family** - the family itself (the default)
- **Possibly family** - not yet sure how they're related
- **Outsiders** - people who matter to the archive without being family
- **All**

## Adding a person

*More* → **Add person**, or **+ Add person** on the people list. Fill in the names and the basics; the rest - birth, death, family, biography - follows on their page, which opens in edit mode.

While you type the names, FMLY3 shows people **already in the archive** with those names. Open one to check it - or, when adding from a family member's page, choose **use this one** to link the existing person instead of making a second one.

## A person's page

- **Names** - given names, the name they were called by, last name at birth, married name, nickname.
- **Born** and **died** - date and place. A date can be exact, *ca.*, *before* or *after*, and day or month can stay empty.
- **Family** - parents, partners, children and siblings, each with their years and places of birth and death - "(1849 Maassluis – 1916 Bandoeng)" - so a move shows down a list of children. Where a row is narrow - two columns, a phone - the places and the surname after "with" make way, and the relation (*Child*, *Half-sibling*) moves under the name.
- **Biography** - their story, in your own words.
- **Photos & documents** they appear in, **comments**, and their **timeline**.

## In edit mode

- **Birth or death record** - *add birth record* / *add death record*. When someone has died but nothing else is known, use **Died, details unknown**. Leave unknown parts empty; never guess.
- **Family** - add a parent, partner or child by searching for them, or make a new person on the spot - type the full name, and the new person's form splits it into given names and last name (check it: "van der Wall" is recognised, a double name without "van" or "de" may need moving). A new relative is family as the person you add them to - family, possibly family or outsider - so the dialog doesn't ask; change it on their page if it differs. When you add a child, choose the **other parent** from your partners, or leave it unset when unknown.
- **Portrait** - the pencil on the avatar: choose one of the photos they're tagged in, then drag, zoom and turn it until their face fills the square.

## The timeline

The timeline puts a life in order: their own events (birth, marriage, divorce, migrations, death), the births and deaths of their close family, and the historical events of their lifetime - each with their age at the time, when both dates are known and certain enough.

An event without a date has no place in time, so it's left out - except the person's own birth (first) and death (last).

## Private people

Some people - usually living relatives - are marked **private**. Their name appears in family lists and on photos, but their page only opens for themselves, their parents and staff.
