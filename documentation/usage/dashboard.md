# The dashboard

The dashboard is where you land after signing in, and where you come back to: the logo in the top left always leads here. It shows what's going on in the archive, and - for editors - what's waiting for you.

From *On this day* to *From the archive*, each block on the left can be **folded**: click its heading. The dashboard remembers which blocks you keep closed.

## On this day

The coming week's anniversaries - births, marriages, deaths and historical events with a known day and month, with how long ago - a teaser; migrations, career steps and the like are on people's pages and in the calendar. When something happened on today's date, it's listed above the week, under its own heading. A living person's birthday shows only for someone signed in.

For the whole year at a glance: **calendar →** next to the heading opens the calendar, at today.

## Recently added and from the archive

- **Recently added** - the newest photos and documents.
- **Family favorites** - what stands out: the items with the most hearts. *Your favorites →* lists your own.
- **From the archive** - one item picked at random, to rediscover something. *Another* picks a new one.

## The conversation

The latest comments, wherever they were made - on a photo, a person or an event. Click one to go to the conversation.

## Your open notes

For editors: your notes still to finish - to-dos, questions, hypotheses. *Notes →* opens them all.

## Your inbox

For editors. New uploads arrive here as drafts, visible only to you until you publish them.

- **Drop files** straight onto the dashboard, or *choose files*. They appear in the inbox as soon as they're uploaded.
- **Open** takes you to the full inbox, where you describe each item and publish it.

See [Photos & documents](content.md) for the inbox in detail.

## Loose ends

For editors: what's still missing in the archive, each as a list with a count, for example:

- people without a birth or without parents, and people born over a hundred years ago without a death (a death without a date counts)
- pictures without people - photos and artworks
- documents without a transcript, and transcripts to finish or check - not identity documents (passport applications, ID cards): they're forms, so tag the people on them and add their facts to those people instead
- events without a date
- people on items without a role - photos and documents with someone on them whose role isn't set yet (*subject*, *depicted*, ...), with how many - set them right there, each with its menu, or with one click to the role the item's kind suggests (*All: depicted* on a group photo) after a look at it. Set the photo or document kind first: an unclassified one suggests nothing. A bar shows how many items are done.
- events without documentation - births, marriages, migrations and the like with no photo or document linked under *documented by*; historical events aren't counted, their source goes in their description. An event whose description cites a source - a link, *Bron:* or *Uit:* ("Bron: IGV: Kleian's Nieuw adresboek - 1913") - is documented outside the archive: it's listed below, under *source cited*, and counts as done. A detail ("Nabij vliegveld Semarang") or a quoted line without its source isn't a source: add *Bron:* to it. The bar shows three parts: documented, source cited, to do. A pill per kind - marriages, births, deaths - shows one kind at a time: a marriage announcement is easier to find than a birth. The same pills on *events without a date*.
- images in low resolution
- places on their own: no parent, and nothing under them - a town that belongs under its country, or a country that's fine as it is
- anything marked with the tag **Loose end** - your own reminders

Where there's a lot of work, a thin bar shows how far along it is - for people (birth and death), documents (transcribed, to check, none) and parents; point at it for the numbers, and the list's own page names the colours. Each list takes you straight to where it's fixed. Something that's as good as it gets - a clipping that won't get sharper, a date that will never be known - can be marked **fine as it is**: it leaves that list (and only that one). *Fine as it is* at the top of a list shows those again, with who decided and why, and puts them back if needed.

## Housekeeping

For staff, under *Housekeeping* in the user menu (top right): the archive's storage, checked by eye. An overview with a count per section, in groups - each section opens on a page of its own:

- **To review** - *revoked*: people, items, events and notes pulled back by staff, by kind, each with its discussion (the revocation reasons and what was said since - a note has none); open one to republish or delete it. *Deleted, to purge*: items, people, events, notes and comments, by kind, to purge for good - after a confirmation that lists what goes along. An item takes its file, thumbnails and parts; a person their family relations and the events only they are in (their birth, their death) - events with others stay, without them; a person with a user account can't be purged. A deleted person, event or note has no page any more; its row shows *admin* (every field) and what it's linked to, still there, to open and check why it was deleted before it goes - a person's family and anyone of the same name (a double?), an event's people, places and proof, a note's links, the page a comment was on (and the comment itself). Links go, the linked records stay. Texts mentioning it keep the words; the link then shows under *mentions that lead nowhere*.
- **Files** - the same file twice, files without a record, records without a file.
- **Records** - *births and deaths that break the rules*: someone with two births or two deaths (the second shows nowhere else), or one birth or death of several people - twins get one each. Normally none. *People linked twice*: someone linked to a document that also documents an event they took part in - the event shows it on their page already. Remove the direct link (the item's ×) where the document is evidence of that moment; keep it where it's theirs. Links with a role that says more (creator, addressee, owner), a frame, or on a photo aren't listed.
- **Tags and places** - unused ones: nothing carries them, nothing lies in them, no text mentions them. Old categories and typos can go; keep what's meant for later.
- **Texts** - *mentions that lead nowhere*: a mention of something deleted for good, or of something revoked or deleted (most readers see only the words). *Remove link* keeps the words and drops the link - or restore what it points to.

Every delete first shows a page with exactly what goes, and is for good - only a backup brings it back.
