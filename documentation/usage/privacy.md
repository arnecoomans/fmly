# Privacy

FMLY3 is built around privacy: an archive of a family holds personal stories, and they're only shown to whom they're meant for.

## Who sees what

Everything in the archive has a **visibility**:

| Visibility | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Anyone, also without signing in |
| **Community** | Everyone with an account - the default |
| **Family** | The people the owner counts as family |
| **Private** | Only the person who added it |

Signed out, only what's explicitly public can be seen: an address of anything else simply isn't found.

## Family

Who counts as your family is your choice: in **Preferences** (your personal menu), tick the accounts of the people you count as family. Your relatives in the family tree are listed first.

It works one way: ticking someone lets them see what *you* mark as family - it doesn't make you part of theirs.

## Drafts

New uploads start as **drafts**: visible only to you until you publish them, so you can complete the information first. Staff can see drafts too, to help when one is forgotten.

## Private people

A person marked **private** - usually a living relative - still appears in family lists and on photos by name, but their page only opens for themselves, their parents and staff.

## Nothing leaves the site

FMLY3 doesn't use outside services. There's no tracking, no analytics and no advertising, and everything - fonts, icons and scripts included - is loaded from FMLY3 itself, so visiting shares nothing with other sites. Text recognition (OCR) runs on FMLY3's own server: your scans never leave it.

Only two cookies are used: one to keep you signed in, and one to protect the forms you send. See the *Cookie statement* at the bottom of every page.

## Deleting

Deleting something hides it from everyone, while it can still be recovered if it was a mistake. Staff can remove it for good later.
