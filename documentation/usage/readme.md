# Using FMLY3

FMLY3 is a family archive and research hub: the people of the family, the photos and documents about them, and the events and places that tie them together. These pages explain how to use it - one page per part of the site.

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md) - an account, signing in, your profile and preferences, edit mode
2. [The dashboard](dashboard.md) - where you start: on this day, your inbox, loose ends
3. [People](people.md) - adding someone, family, birth and death, portraits, the timeline
4. [Photos & documents](content.md) - uploading, describing, pages, viewing, transcribing
5. [Events and places](events-and-places.md) - life events, history, the calendar, places
6. [Research](research.md) - notes, search, comments
7. [Privacy](privacy.md) - who sees what
