# Getting started

## An account

Most of the archive is only visible to family members who are signed in. Without an account you see a welcome page - and only what was deliberately made public.

- On the welcome page, choose **Request an account**.
- Staff approve new accounts by hand. Until then you can't sign in.
- Once approved, sign in with your username and password.

Accounts come in two kinds:

- **Visitors** can see what's shared with them and join the conversation (comments).
- **Editors** can also add and change the archive: people, photos and documents, events, places and notes.

## The header

Always at the top of the page:

- **Dashboard**, **People** and **Photos & documents** - the main parts of the archive.
- **More** - events, comments, tags, places and notes; for editors also *Add content*, *Add person* and your *Inbox*.
- **Search** - searches the whole archive (see [Research](research.md)).
- **Edit** - for editors: switches edit mode on and off (see below).
- Your initials - your personal menu.

On a phone the header shows icons only.

## Your personal menu

Click your initials in the top right:

- **My record** - your own page in the family, when your account is linked to it.
- **Profile** - your name and email address.
- **Preferences** - your language, who you count as family (see [Privacy](privacy.md)), and whether you're superstitious: then people with an exact birth date show their star sign.
- **Log out.**

Staff see a second group, *Staff*, with tools to maintain the archive.

## Following a trail

From a tag, place, event or person, the page you open next highlights it - the tag among a photo's tags, the place in its places, the person among an event's people - outlined. It shows how you got there, and it's the way back. One step only: the page after that is plain again.

## Edit mode

Editors read the archive like everyone else. To change something, press **Edit** in the header: the button turns orange and pencils appear next to everything you may change.

- Click a **pencil** to edit that part in place; **Save** keeps it, **Cancel** leaves it as it was.
- Choices like a kind or a visibility are **buttons**: a click saves.
- Press **Editing** again to stop. Unsaved changes are pointed out first.

Edit mode stays on while you move through the site, so you can work through several pages in a row.
