Welcome to FMLY 3.0: a whole new content design, focusing on content- and person-centric research.

**FMLY3** is a content- and person-centered family archive and research hub. Store your old photos, annotate who's on them and discover the family around these people. Build a family database: store who was born when and where and who married whom, but also the proof and the stories behind it. FMLY3 builds the missing link between a photo archive and a family tree.

## Dashboard
New in FMLY: start from one central place.
- **On this day** - births, marriages, deaths and history that happened on today's date; on a quiet day, the coming week. The calendar shows the whole year.
- **Recently added** and **from the archive** - what's new, and a random item to rediscover.
- **The conversation** - the latest comments, wherever they were made.
- **Your inbox** - drop files straight onto the dashboard; they wait as drafts until you've described them.
- **Loose ends** - what's still missing: people without a birth or a portrait, photos without people, documents without a transcript, events without a date. Each list takes you straight to where it's fixed, and anything that's as good as it gets can be marked "fine as it is".
- **You in the archive** - your own record, and what you've added.

## People
In the people section you add the family members. Store the basics like names, birth and death records, parents, partners and children. Use events to record life events like engagements, marriages and migrations. Together this builds a family structure and a timeline for each person.

## Content
"Photos & documents" holds the photos and documents that record the lives and the surroundings of the family. Store that family group photo and research who is on it. Add a date and a location to complete the context. Add documents too, like newspaper clippings or civil records, to give the lives depth and context. Transcribe the documents for readability and searchability.

## Events and Places
Events and places have their own spot in the archive. They link people and content together and put them in order. Find the news stories and history around the people you're interested in.

## Research
- **Notes** - keep your questions, leads, hypotheses and conclusions next to the people, photos and events they're about. A question can grow into a conclusion as you find out more.
- **Search** - one search across people, photos and documents, events, notes, places and tags - including the text of transcripts and biographies.
- **Comments** - add a memory or ask the family about a photo, a person or an event. Everyone who can see it can join in.

## Privacy
FMLY3 is built around privacy. Content is "community-visible" by default: only signed-in users can see it. More personal content can be marked "family-visible", to share it with the people you count as family in your preferences, or "private", to keep it to yourself. Only content explicitly marked "public" can be seen without signing in.

New uploads start as drafts, visible only to you until you publish them - so you can complete the information before sharing. No worries: staff can always help you here.

FMLY3 doesn't use external services that could share your information. There's no tracking, no analytics and no advertising, and everything - fonts, icons, Bootstrap and jQuery included - is loaded from FMLY3 itself, so visiting shares nothing with other sites.
