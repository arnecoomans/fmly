{% load content_tags %} {% comment %} One content item as a .content-card (components.css), linking to its detail page. Cross-app: the person page's Content section today, the content list later. Composition by what can be shown, not by kind: an image file (a photo, but also a scanned document) gets the thumbnail-forward card, with the 'card' preset via content:thumbnail; anything else - a PDF, audio, a book without a cover, a record whose file isn't supplied yet - gets the icon-forward card. data-filter-value carries the kind, for the filter pills (cmnsd.js filter.js); data-sort-added / data-sort-date feed the sort switch (sort.js). A draft (or revoked) item - only its creator and staff see it - is marked: .content-card--draft, a dashed frame and its status as a badge. An item with a transcript (item.transcript) shows a text icon, top right - outlined when it's marked incomplete. An item with parts (item.has_parts, set by the grid's mark_wholes) gets a count badge - .content-card--has-parts: the whole plus its parts (item.group_size), as on the item's page.
